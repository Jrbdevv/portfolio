The Brief

BigBattery wanted its own flagship in the lithium home-energy market: a battery for off-grid homeowners who want to be self-sufficient, running on their own solar and their own storage.

I was the only product designer on it, and on every product that followed. The units start from a supplier's base hardware, and my work was everything a buyer sees and touches on the outside. The first NEXUS set the look the rest of the line was built on: the inverters that turned it into a complete system, then a second generation.

My Role

  • Exterior design on the supplier's base unit: panel measurements, tolerances and label placement
  • Color: the Pantone specification for the casing and the branding
  • Concept iteration across multiple rounds of mockups, through executive review to approval
  • Packaging: the retail box for the APEX inverters, designed with AI and CorelDRAW
  • Launch: product animation, website infographics, sale banners and email creative
  • Coordination with the B2B and marketing teams, so product, packaging and launch shipped together

The exterior

Thinner than a Powerwall and designed for Americans to own their power.

At 5.91 inches deep, RAIO is slimmer than Tesla’s Powerwall 3 at 7.6 inches. I designed a sleek cabinet with a premium glass front panel, in Stealth Gray or Pearl White, that mounts on a wall or stands on the floor. The cutaway shows how it stays that thin: the inverter, the battery management system and two battery modules share one enclosure.

The BigBattery app pairs with it, so homeowners can watch and run their own energy instead of depending on someone else for their power. That's the idea behind BigBattery's motto: Own Your Power.

The product page

No more researching batteries and inverters. Just one all-in-one system.

The page leads with a finish and a configuration rather than a spec sheet. Buyers pick Stealth Gray or Pearl White, then one to five units, with capacity and price updating as they go. A system customizer starts from the job (grid-tied, off-grid, golf cart, RV, marine or industrial) and suggests the inverter and batteries for it, and everything in the box is listed before the add-to-cart button.
